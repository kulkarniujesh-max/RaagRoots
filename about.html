THE PEOPLE BEHIND RAAGROOTS

Three minds.
One love for Indian music.

RaagRoots is our project celebrating the instruments, craftsmanship, and musical traditions that make India's sound so distinctive. We're bringing these instruments into one welcoming space where every sound has a story.

♫
OUR PURPOSE

Rooted in tradition.
Made for discovery.

We created RaagRoots to make exploring Indian musical instruments feel simple, inspiring, and accessible. From familiar favourites to instruments you may be discovering for the first time, our goal is to help people connect with the sound, heritage, and artistry behind each one. This website is a student project built through teamwork, creativity, and a shared appreciation for music.
